About this page: statistics are computed in your browser from the 883 cases recorded on Sheet1 of “ITSC team ticket form (25-26 Semester 2).xlsx” (12 Jan 2026 – 23 Jul 2026), last read on 31 Oct 2026. Totals are computed by this page from the full Sheet1 transcription: 883 total cases · 883 Resolved + 0 On Going (100.0%) · 242 incidents · average handling time 41.6 minutes. This workbook has no Dashboard sheet. Only statistics fields are published here; reporter emails, names and free-text details from the form are not included. Values are reproduced as written in the form. Staff: where the form lists several names for a case, the case is counted under the first-named staff member. This is a static archive page — the semester is closed and the data is not refreshed.
