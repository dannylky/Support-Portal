v1.0

Ticket Statistics launched

30 Oct 2026

Data: 275 cases · 1 Sep – 30 Oct 2026 · Sep 180 / Oct 95 · read in full from Sheet1 of 'ITSC team ticket form (26-27 Semester 1).xlsx'
