v1.20

Dark mode becomes a portal-wide setting

14 Nov 2026

Data: 299 cases · Sep 180 · Oct 95 · Nov 24 (data unchanged)

  • Dark mode moved out of this page's hero and into the Support Portal navigation bar: the 🌙/☀️ toggle there now controls dark mode on every portal page.
  • This page keeps its full native dark theme (including chart colours); it is now driven by the shared portal preference (localStorage sp-theme), and the previous ts-dark setting is migrated automatically.
  • The old hero dark-mode button is retired — the navigation toggle is the single control.

Verification: Local rebuild; browser checks: nav toggle switches this page's dark theme and chart colours, the preference persists across portal pages (home, Remote Campus, Annual Plan), and the retired hero button no longer shows.
