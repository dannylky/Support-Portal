About this page: statistics are computed in your browser from the 275 cases recorded on Sheet1 of “ITSC team ticket form (26-27 Semester 1).xlsx” (1 Sep 2026 – 30 Oct 2026), last read on 1 Nov 2026. At every refresh the totals are cross-checked against the workbook's own Dashboard: 275 total cases · 274 Resolved + 1 On Going (99.6%) · 131 incidents · average handling time 24.0 minutes — all match. Only statistics fields are published here; reporter emails, names and free-text details from the form are not included. Two category labels were width-truncated in the sheet view (“Classroom - System po…”) and are shown as “Classroom - System power”. “Classroom - Touch Pane” is reproduced as written in the form.
