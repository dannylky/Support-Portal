About this page: statistics are computed in your browser from the 1434 cases recorded on Sheet1 of “ITSC team ticket form (24-25 Semester 1).xlsx” (2 Sep 2024 – 3 Jan 2025), last read on 23 Nov 2026. Totals are computed by this page from the full Sheet1 transcription: 1434 total cases · 1418 Resolved + 16 On Going (98.9%) · 297 incidents · average handling time 32.9 minutes. This workbook has no Dashboard sheet. Only statistics fields are published here; reporter emails, names and free-text details from the form are not included. Values are reproduced as written in the form. Staff: where the form lists several names for a case, the case is counted under the first-named staff member. Semester 1 data is drawn from the four monthly workbooks (Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec 2024); the December workbook also holds the 18 cases dated January 2025. The September 2024 sheet lists Ids 242-253 twice; the duplicated rows are counted as recorded. This is a static archive page — the semester is closed and the data is not refreshed.
